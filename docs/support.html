Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I import a configuration profile?

You can import configuration profiles by tapping the "+" button in the top corner of the Locations tab. YPtun supports scanning QR codes directly with your camera or pasting share links and JSON subscription configurations from your clipboard.

Why does YPtun ask for VPN configuration permission?

iOS requires system authorization to establish a local network tunnel using Apple's Network Extension API. This allows YPtun to securely encrypt and route your connection packets according to your chosen profile settings.

How does latency (ping) testing work?

When you tap on a server or press the ping test button, YPtun sends a small, lightweight handshake probe to test round-trip latency (RTT) in milliseconds. This helps you select the fastest and most stable connection available.

How do I control YPtun from Control Center or Widgets?

On iOS 18+, you can add the YPtun toggle button directly to your Control Center or Lock Screen shortcuts. Interactive widgets are also available on your Home Screen and in Dynamic Island (Live Activity) when connected.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please send an email to yptunn@gmail.com detailing your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue. We appreciate your feedback!
