Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 • Version 1.0

✓ Strict Zero-Logs Commitment

This Privacy Policy describes how YPtun ("we", "our", or "the app") treats user privacy and data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your network traffic and personal data.

Core Principle: YPtun does not collect, record, track, log, or share your browsing history, traffic destinations, DNS queries, data payloads, or IP addresses. We have designed YPtun as a strictly decentralized client-side network utility.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1 and Guideline 5.4 for VPN Applications):

  • No Traffic Logs: We do not log what websites you visit, services you use, or data packets you transmit.
  • No Connection Timestamps: We do not store records of when you connect or disconnect.
  • No IP Address Logging: We do not record your real IP address or the destination IP address of your connections.
  • No DNS Logs: We do not record or store your DNS queries.
  • No Account Information: YPtun requires no account registration, no login, no email address, and no phone number.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: The app does not integrate third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, or profiling SDKs.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data and settings required for the operation of YPtun are stored strictly locally on your device:

  • Tunnel Configurations: Server addresses, port numbers, public/private keys, and routing rules imported by you are stored securely in local device storage (such as iOS Keychain and sandboxed UserDefaults / App Group).
  • Diagnostic Metrics: Network latency (ping) and packet transfer counters displayed in the app or Live Activity are calculated locally in real-time in memory and are discarded when the session ends.

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any proprietary servers owned by YPtun.

3. Network Permissions (Apple Network Extension)

To provide secure tunneling functionality on iOS, YPtun uses Apple's official NetworkExtension framework (specifically NEPacketTunnelProvider). This permission allows the app to establish a local virtual network interface and direct network packets through your configured tunnels.

All network processing is carried out according to the protocol configurations provided or selected by the user.

4. Third-Party Servers and Endpoints

YPtun functions as a client. If you configure and connect to a third-party server or VPS, your encrypted traffic is routed through that specific server. The privacy practices of that independent server provider will apply to your network traffic on that endpoint. We strongly recommend using trusted servers and infrastructure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected, no parental consent is required under COPPA or similar international regulations.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or regulatory compliance. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date".
