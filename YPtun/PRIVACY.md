# Privacy Policy for YPtun

**Effective Date:** October 11, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 11, 2026

YPtun ("the Application") is an open, client-side network proxy and routing utility designed to provide users with direct, secure, and encrypted connections to user-specified servers. We strongly believe in fundamental privacy rights and data sovereignty.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection Policy
- **No Personal Information:** YPtun does not require registration, login credentials, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No Browsing Activity Logging:** YPtun does not inspect, collect, monitor, record, or store any network traffic, visited URLs, DNS queries, packet contents, or connection metadata.
- **No IP Address or Location Tracking:** We do not track, log, or associate your physical location, device IP addresses, or device identifiers with your usage.

### 2. Local Data Storage
- All network configurations, server profiles (VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, SOCKS5), routing rules, and preferences are stored **exclusively on your local device** within the application's secure sandbox.
- Sensitive values (e.g., credentials and cryptographic keys) are protected by iOS/iPadOS/macOS system security mechanisms (Apple Keychain).
- This data is never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

### 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics
- **No Analytics SDKs:** YPtun does not include third-party tracking, analytics, or behavioural profiling SDKs (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK).
- **No Advertisements:** YPtun contains zero advertising frameworks and does not share data with ad networks.
- **Direct Traffic Routing:** When an encrypted tunnel is established, network packets flow strictly between your device and the destination endpoints/servers specified by you. The developer does not operate or control intermediate proxy logging infrastructure.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
YPtun does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or software updates. Any updates will be published directly in this repository.
